# Commander artwork

Both assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The app icon is
stored as `CommanderIcon.heic` at 1024 × 1024. The build converts it into a
multi-resolution `.icns` for Commander and Setup. `Keydial.heic` is the
transparent device artwork used by the editor and Settings Preview.

## App icon prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. New polished macOS app icon for Keydial Commander, a Huion K20 shortcut-pad utility. Square 1:1 composition, genuinely transparent outside a large rounded-square app tile occupying 88% of the canvas. Premium graphite-to-midnight blue anodized squircle, brushed silver circular dial in the upper left, elegantly simplified dark macro keys below arranged in clean regular rows, subtle electric cyan illuminated edge/ring and refined studio highlights. Strong clean silhouette and clear at 16px. Highly crafted macOS app icon with tactile metallic detail, almost straight-on with minimal perspective; no lettering, logo, or watermark.

## Keypad artwork prompt

> Use case: product-mockup. Make a new photographic-quality isolated product rendering of the black Huion Keydial Mini K20 from the attached reference (reference is for the device silhouette and exact geometry only, ignore all text/callout overlays and background). Strict flat straight top-down orthographic view with no perspective or rotation, entire product closely fills frame with a little transparent padding, vertical 4:6.45 device aspect ratio. One large metal silver dial with a small dark circular center in upper left of header. Exactly 18 black blank keycaps in a 4-column regular grid under header: rows 1,2,3 each four normal square keys; row 4 has three normal square keys then one tall key spanning rows 4–5 in the far right column; bottom row has one double-width horizontal key spanning columns 1–2 and one regular square key in column 3. No other keys. Header roughly 26% of product height. Black graphite plastic body, authentic studio light and clean material, no text, no key legends, no arrows, no logo, no hands, no background, no shadow outside silhouette. Accurate key placement matters because software overlays clickable regions on this image.
